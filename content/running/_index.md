I run alone, early, while the city is still quiet. Not for performance and not really for health, though I'll take it. Running is just running. It's easy and it's hard. It's the one thing I look forward to when I close my eyes at night, and the first thing I negotiate my way out of when the alarm goes.

When the run happens, the whole day works. When I start with the phone instead, everything goes downhill from there. I have known this for about ten years. It hasn't stopped me checking the phone first.

**What I wanted in 2022**, kept here as a public record of my optimism:

- Run earlier: 5:17 in the morning.
- Never get injured.
- Understand the mechanics of running.
- A new course every month.
- 42 km twice, 21 km four times.
- 10 km a day for seven days in a row.

I managed some of these. I'm not saying which.
