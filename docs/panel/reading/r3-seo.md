## 3. IDEA: the card remembers (WOW lift)
**"Checked out."** Stamps you cause stay, as faint ghost impressions.
- **Trigger:** pointerenter or focus on a row stamps it as specified (120ms). On leave the stamp does not vanish; it settles to 0.35 opacity over 200ms and stays in the date column (never over text).
- **Counter:** a mono line under the h1 counts up on each new stamp: `17 of 104 checked out`. Text only, no animation.
- **Reset:** a "Clear the card" link (also `Esc` when no row is open) removes all ghosts instantly.
- **Cap:** at most 12 ghosts animate their settle at once; the rest are set instantly. Transform and opacity only.
- **Fallback:** no JS means CSS `:hover` and `:focus-within` stamp only, with no persistence. Reduced motion means ghost state is applied instantly.
- **Why it wows:** a long scroll turns the page into a card covered in your own red ink. The 2019 card with 27 books becomes a visible trail of where you lingered, which is shareable as a screenshot. It uses one grammar, input-only motion, no cover colours, and costs a single class toggle per row (no layout reads, no rAF).
